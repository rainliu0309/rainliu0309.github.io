Project Overview & Organizational Pain Point

项目概述与组织痛点

Agent-powered collaboration solution targeting structural cross-team alignment gaps in project delivery.

基于Agent的协作方案，解决项目交付中结构性的跨团队对齐缺口。

Problem Framing

问题定义

Cross-functional project delivery suffers from information silos: meeting knowledge is trapped in audio, action items and risks rely on manual extraction, causing constant re-alignment cost and unexposed delivery hazards.

跨职能项目交付存在信息孤岛：会议知识沉淀在录音内，任务与风险依赖人工整理，持续产生对齐成本、风险难以前置暴露。

Root Pain Classification

痛点归类

  1. Knowledge capture cost

    知识捕获成本

    Manual transcription & item sorting creates repetitive administrative load

    人工纪要与任务整理带来重复事务开销

  2. Asynchronous visibility gap

    异步可视缺口

    Risk and accountability remain implicit across departments

    风险、责任在跨部门流转中隐性化

  3. Low consensus persistence

    共识留存薄弱

    Verbal agreements fail to form traceable project baseline

    口头共识无法形成可追溯的项目基准

    互联网类中小企业内部跨职能团队

    产品边界
