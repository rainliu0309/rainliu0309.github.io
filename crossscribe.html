01 Project Overview & Business Background 项目概述与业务背景

A lightweight RAG-powered SaaS for cross-border merchants, built around manual content and compliance pain points.

面向中小跨境卖家的轻量化 RAG AI SaaS，聚焦人工文案与合规流程的效率痛点。

02 Market Research & Competitor Analysis 市场调研与竞品分析

From market demand and competitor gaps to a focused RAG entry point and defensible product barriers.

从市场机会与竞品缺口出发，明确 RAG 切入点与可持续产品壁垒。

Market Opportunity

市场机会

Cross-border sellers need efficient, compliant marketing tools.

跨境卖家急需高效合规的营销内容工具。

Amazon listings are easily removed for rule violations, and Shopify content lacks unified brand tone, bringing huge operational pressure to sellers.

亚马逊商品链接易因违规被平台下架，独立站店铺文案缺乏统一品牌调性，给卖家带来极大运营压力。

Competitor Gap

竞品缺口

Existing tools lack vertical and compliance capabilities.

现有工具缺少跨境垂直能力与合规意识。

General AI tools, rigid cross-border templates, and basic translation services all fail to meet sellers' needs for compliant, brand-aligned, high-converting copy.

通用AI工具、固化跨境模板工具与基础翻译工具，均无法满足卖家对合规、贴合品牌调性且具备高转化力文案的需求。

Our Entry Point

我方切入点

Focus on RAG, private knowledge base and compliance.

聚焦RAG、私有知识库与合规驱动文案生成。

Import brand materials, product parameters and platform rules into the database. The AI automatically references standards during generation to ensure compliant and brand-consistent copy.

将卖家专属品牌资料、产品参数、平台规则入库，AI生成文案时自动对标标准，保证内容合规且贴合品牌调性。

Product Barrier

产品壁垒

Build private knowledge, compliance rules and scenario templates.

构建私有知识、合规规则与场景模板壁垒。

Continuously accumulate platform rule templates and verified high-quality copy data. This vertical industry data cannot be replicated by general AI tools.

持续沉淀平台规则模板与验证过的优质文案数据，这套垂直行业数据是通用AI工具无法复刻的核心优势。
